An Empirical Chronicle with Longitudinal Data · Brazil, 2021 to 2026

Brazil insists on discussing giftedness. Individual creators answer; institutions remain silent.

Across a six-year observation window, discourse migrated from standard IQ psychometrics to mobile devices, clinical consultation offices, and statutory legislation. I measured every sequential transition across three scalar dimensions: reach, likes, and comments.

Contextual Baseline

A Discourse Expanding Simultaneously Across All Empirical Fronts

In 2021, mainstream journalism published 53 headlines addressing giftedness. By 2026, that figure surged to 411. Across social platforms—and this represents the mathematically compelling phenomenon—the expansion factor proved substantially greater.

I aggregated on-topic YouTube and TikTok videos alongside Instagram publications. The top calibration control toggles the measurement metric: reach (video views), likes, or comments. All visualizations update synchronously, ensuring strictly homogeneous scalar comparisons.

Act I · The Psychometric Digit

First, IQ Was Transformed into Spectacle

At the genesis of our observation window, giftedness was treated as strictly synonymous with a psychometric scalar. "Genius" trivia quizzes, optical illusions, and viral profiles claiming the "highest IQ in Brazil" captured disproportionate public attention. Entirely predictable. Numbers are fascinating—prime numbers particularly so, though that is a treatise for another day.

In 2021, nearly half of all analyzed YouTube comments (47%) consisted of self-deprecating satire regarding personal IQ. By 2026, that proportion plummeted to 17%. Sensationalism still commands reach, but the public began formulating an epistemologically superior query: does the test actually measure a valid construct?

Act II · The Reflective Mirror

Subsequently, It Transmuted into Identity

Between 2023 and 2024, TikTok and Instagram replaced the trivial query "what is your IQ?" with a vastly more introspective question: "do you recognize yourself in these behavioral traits?". A genuine paradigm shift, in the strictest epistemological sense formulated by Thomas Kuhn.

Psychologists, neuropsychologists, and physicians cataloged clinical traits, discussing sensory overexcitabilities, ADHD and autism comorbidity (twice-exceptionality), and late-identified adults. The public responded unequivocally, and the empirical telemetry records it: childhood & family and schooling represent the subjects commanding peak organic reach.

Act III · Statutory Codification

In 2026, Discourse Reached the State Apparatus

In February, a six-year-old child prodigy addressed the Federal Senate. In June, Federal Law 15,436 instituted the national public policy for gifted and twice-exceptional students. Within four calendar months, the issue moved from the Senate podium to executive presidential sanction. By legislative bureaucracy standards, that is practically relativistic light speed.

Public policy surged from 6% of news reports in 2021 to 26% in 2026—a greater than fourfold increase in proportional share. Across all harvested multimedia items, the video of the child addressing the Senate amassed the highest volume of public comments.

The Turning Point

The Most Frequently Cited Institution Reaches the Fewest Individuals

Mensa constitutes the single most frequently cited organization within this subject domain. Over this observation window, Mensa Brasil reported an expansion from 2,000 to 7,000 members, serving as the default primary source for press accounts covering prodigies.

On social platforms, however, Mensa's own direct voice is practically undetectable. Furthermore, when the public mentions the society in comment sections, the vast majority express explicit criticism. The grievances recur with near-experimental regularity: exclusionary club, prohibitive testing fees, perceived intellectual elitism.

The Climax

Behind the Sarcastic Facade: Genuine Inquiry and Human Suffering

I examined comments formulating inquiries or sharing personal anguish. In fully 4 out of every 10, one identifies authentic psychological confusion or distress. This is not statistical noise: it represents human beings.

Virtually none inquire about formal institutions. Queries center on acute suffering, diagnostic conflation with ADHD and autism, chronic social alienation, and the haunting dilemma "could I be gifted?". Today, clinical digital creators answer these queries, while advocacy institutions remain conspicuously silent.
